# PNL-01 bounded acceptance

This append-only package records the project owner's bounded financial
acceptance of realized P&L for the exact real-broker lifecycle scopes already
accepted under ONJ-TRUST-01B, ONJ-TRUST-02, ONJ-TRUST-03, ONJ-TRUST-04, and
ONJ-TRUST-06.

The aggregate attestation does not replace or broaden those proofs. It binds
their public owner-acceptance and evidence-use records by SHA-256 and preserves
all case-specific limitations. No new private evidence bundle is required
because this record introduces no new broker evidence, calculation, or
rehearsal result.

The acceptance is limited to the named proof scopes. It excludes real-exercise
acceptance, closure of the ONJ-TRUST-06 replacement contract, unresolved
`review_required` or unapproved description-only events, complete account
history, portfolio-wide financial correctness, unrealized P&L or valuation,
complete ADR-0010 provenance, production readiness, and any authority for
provider, database, migration, deployment, push, or trading actions.

`COMPLETE` in the roadmap records completion of the bounded PNL-01 delivery
evidence. It does not establish runtime availability, operational acceptance,
portfolio-wide acceptance, or production readiness.
